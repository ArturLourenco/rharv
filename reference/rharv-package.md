# rharv: Daily Water-Balance Simulation of Rainwater Harvesting Systems

Simulates rainwater harvesting reservoirs with a daily water-balance
(mass-balance) model. From a daily precipitation series it computes the
captured volume, runs the reservoir balance and derives performance
metrics and guarantee-based sizing values (constant demand, catchment
area and reservoir capacity). The available-volume relation and the
daily-simulation approach follow established references (e.g. Souza,
2015; ABNT NBR 15527). Aimed at water-security analysis of buildings,
especially in semi-arid regions.
